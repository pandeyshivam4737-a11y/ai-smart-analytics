Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning are transforming modern data processing pipelines at unprecedented speed. Large Language Models (LLMs) allow organizations to extract actionable insights from unstructured textual data with high accuracy. However, efficient deployment requires optimized algorithms and minimal latency. By leveraging specialized data structures such as Inverted Indexes and Priority Queues, software engineers can achieve sub-millisecond retrieval times even over millions of documents. Furthermore, combining rule-based heuristics with deep learning architectures delivers reliable, explainable results for mission-critical enterprise systems. In conclusion, mastering computational efficiency alongside AI models is the hallmark of modern software craftsmanship.
